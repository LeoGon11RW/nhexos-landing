Before we start

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need a finished specification to get started?

No. Share your idea, intended users, and the problem you want to solve. Discovery can help turn that starting point into a defined scope.

Can you work with our existing application?

Tell us about your current technology and constraints. An integration or migration approach can be scoped after reviewing the application and its dependencies.

How are security and data privacy handled?

Access controls, data handling, provider settings, and security requirements need to be agreed during scoping. Avoid sending passwords, API keys, or sensitive customer data in an initial inquiry.

How long will development take?

The timeline depends on scope, integrations, and review cycles. Milestones and delivery estimates are defined in the project proposal.

Is this a subscription? Can I cancel?

These services are quoted by project. Billing, changes, cancellation, and any ongoing support are governed by the agreed proposal and contract.

Will you provide support after launch?

Discuss your support needs during discovery. Maintenance, response expectations, documentation, and handover should be specified in the proposal.
